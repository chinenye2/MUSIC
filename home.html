THE HIGH LIFE REVOLUTIONARY

ROOTS. RHYTHM. REBIRTH.

Where heritage meets the future of African sound.

Born Chimaobi Martins Abonyi, the Nigerian artist is developing a sound that sits at the intersection of contemporary Afrosounds, Afro-fusion and the timeless spirit of Highlife. His music carries the energy of modern African pop while remaining connected to culture, language, spirituality and the stories that have shaped generations.

For Quti, music is not simply entertainment. It is identity.
